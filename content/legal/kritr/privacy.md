## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

### What Are Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data placed on your device by websites you visit. They help websites recognize your browser and remember your preferences.

### How We Use Cookies

- **Strictly Necessary Cookies**: Required for basic website functionality such as setting your privacy preferences. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information
- **Analytics Cookies**: We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our websites. These tools collect information such as page views, session duration, and navigation patterns in aggregated form
- **Marketing Cookies**: We may use cookies to measure the effectiveness of our marketing efforts

### Your Cookie Choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You may also manage your preferences through our cookie consent tool, if available on the website. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
